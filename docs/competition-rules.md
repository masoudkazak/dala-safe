## 3. Eligibility — critical interpretation

The eligibility rule applies to the **participant**, not to the project's target
market. Dala Safe is a Kazakhstan-focused product, but that does not by itself
make the submitter eligible.

A submission is eligible only if the submitter can truthfully confirm all of:

- [ ] Is an ethnic Kazakh (the regulation says «ұлты қазақ отандастар» /
      “ethnic Kazakhs”).
- [ ] Is aged 15–30 at submission.
- [ ] Is a citizen of a foreign country (not a citizen of Kazakhstan).
- [ ] Can provide full name, citizenship, university and year of study, and age.

If any item is false, do not submit under these rules; ask the organizer first.
Do not distort the founder or participant identity to fit the rule. This is an
integrity requirement, not a formatting detail.

The rule does **not** say the project must be physically located abroad, must be
incorporated abroad, or must target diaspora users. That question is not
answered by the regulation; see U-10.

## 4. Presentation deliverable

The regulation offers a choice: **video OR slide deck**. The PDF description is
separate and always required.

### 4.1 Video (recommended for Dala Safe)

| Attribute | Required value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Horizontal / landscape | Verified |
| Maximum duration | 2 minutes (120 seconds) | Verified |
| Container | MP4 or MOV per English text; `MP3` is a likely typo in KK/RU | Verified with conflict |
| Content | Project content, relevance, objective, target audience, implementation methods, expected outcomes | Verified |
| Resolution, codec, bitrate, max size | Not specified | Unverified |

Production decision (not a rule): export H.264/AAC MP4, 1920×1080, 25 or 30 fps,
audio clearly audible, and verify duration ≤ 120.0 s with a media probe before
sending. Do not rely on `MP3`; an audio-only file cannot satisfy the
“video” requirement.

### 4.2 Slide deck (alternative)

- PPTX or PDF.
- No more than 10 slides.
- Must cover the same presentation content as the video.

If a video is submitted, the slide deck is not required. Sending both is not
prohibited, but it is also not described; see U-09.

## 5. Written PDF description

### 5.1 Mandatory sections

The PDF must contain, at minimum, these nine items in a clear order:

1. Project title.
2. Relevance of the project and the problem it addresses.
3. Project objective.
4. Key project tasks.
5. Target audience.
6. Implementation mechanism and key activities.
7. Project implementation period.
8. Expected outcomes.
9. Resources required for implementation.

For an operating project: current results, achievements, and further development
plans. For a planned project: objective, tasks, stages, timeline, and expected
outcomes. Dala Safe is a partially built MVP, so the PDF should clearly separate
**implemented**, **tested**, **simulated/demo**, **planned**, and
**integration-dependent** work. This is required by the master prompt and is
consistent with the competition's “current results” clause.

### 5.2 Budget

- A budget is required **only if funding is requested**.
- If requested: state the total amount and a detailed budget.
- Each line: expense name, quantity, unit cost, total cost.
- Currency is not specified in the rules. Use KZT and state the currency
  explicitly; do not mix currencies without a conversion note.
- Do not invent costs. Use quotes or documented market prices and label
  estimates as estimates.

The master prompt additionally asks for stages/timeline, dependencies, risks,
metrics, and future plans. These are allowed as extra sections and improve the
submission, but they do not replace the nine mandatory items.

## 6. Submission mechanics (verified)

1. Send to **Oqbaiqau@gmail.com**.
2. Attach the presentation (video **or** deck) and the PDF description.
3. Include in the submission: full name, citizenship, university and year of
   study, age.
4. Submit by **2026-10-30**.
5. Language: Kazakh, Russian, or English.
6. Submission implies consent to publication on the Foundation website and its
   Facebook, Instagram, and Telegram accounts.

Not specified: subject line, file naming, file size caps, whether personal data
must be in the email body or inside the PDF, whether a receipt is issued, and
whether a cloud link is accepted. Treat all of these as unverified.

## 7. UNVERIFIED and ambiguous rules

These are **not rules**. They are open questions that must be resolved before
submission. Do not encode any of them as facts in the PDF, video, or app.

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Required action before submission |
|---|---|---|---|
| U-01 | Is the video container MP4 or MP3? EN says MP4; KK and RU say MP3. | An audio-only MP3 would fail as a “video”. | Use MP4 and confirm with the organizer by email. Keep the reply. |
| U-02 | What exact time and timezone closes the deadline on 2026-10-30? | A late send is a total loss. | Ask by email; plan to send by 2026-10-29 18:00 Asia/Almaty regardless. |
| U-03 | Is there a maximum PDF size or page count? | Large PDFs may bounce. | Ask; keep PDF under ~10 MB and verify email attachment limits. |
| U-04 | Is there a required email subject line or file naming convention? | Unnamed files complicate matching. | Ask; otherwise use `OQ_Startup_2026_DalaSafe_Presentation.mp4` and `OQ_Startup_2026_DalaSafe_Description.pdf`. |
| U-05 | Are team submissions allowed, or only individual participants? | Changes who is named and who signs consent. | Ask before listing a team. |
| U-06 | Is “university and year of study” mandatory for a non-student applicant? | Could block an otherwise eligible founder. | Ask; if not a student, state status honestly and request guidance. |
| U-07 | What are the judging criteria and weights? | Affects emphasis in the PDF and video. | Ask; do not invent criteria. |
| U-08 | May a live product URL or demo link be included? | Helpful but not part of the stated format. | Ask; include only as an extra if confirmed. |
| U-09 | May both a video and a slide deck be submitted? | The rule says “or”, not “and”. | Send video + PDF by default; ask before adding a deck. |
| U-10 | Must the project itself be connected to Kazakhstan or the diaspora? | The text is silent; do not assume. | Ask if the project's Kazakhstan focus raises any issue. |
| U-11 | Must the video and PDF be in the same language? | Mixing may complicate judging. | Choose one language for both unless told otherwise. |
| U-12 | Is submission receipt confirmed, and how? | No confirmation means no proof of delivery. | Request a read receipt and, if possible, written confirmation. |
| U-13 | What are the intellectual-property and publication terms beyond the consent clause? | Publication is broad. | Ask for the full terms if the project contains proprietary code or branding. |
| U-14 | Can previous OQ Startup winners or previous applicants re-enter? | Affects eligibility. | Ask. |
| U-15 | Is the budget currency fixed, and are taxes/VAT/overheads allowed? | Affects the credibility of the budget. | Ask; otherwise state KZT, taxes, and assumptions explicitly. |
| U-16 | Is a budget mandatory even when no funding is requested? | The text says “if funding is required”. | If no funding is requested, state that explicitly in the PDF. |

## 8. Phase 7 re-verification checklist

Run this checklist on the day of submission, not earlier. Rules may change.

### 8.1 Re-read the official regulation

- [ ] Open https://oq.gov.kz/en/news/1564 and confirm: deadline, email, age,
      citizenship, languages, video duration/orientation, PDF sections.
- [ ] Open https://oq.gov.kz/ru/news/1564 and https://oq.gov.kz/kz/news/1564
      and diff them against the English text.
- [ ] Confirm whether the MP4/MP3 conflict is still present.
- [ ] Save dated PDF/screenshot copies of all three language versions.

### 8.2 Check for updates or extensions

- [ ] Check https://oq.gov.kz/press/ and the announcements section for a newer
      OQ Startup post.
- [ ] Check https://t.me/s/otandastar for a newer post or deadline change.
- [ ] Check Instagram `@otandastar_qory` and Facebook `OtandastarQory` if
      accessible.
- [ ] Check https://memleket.kz for an updated embassy repost.
- [ ] If any source differs from S1, the newest official post wins; record the
      change in section 10.

### 8.3 Resolve unknowns by email

- [ ] Send one concise email to Oqbaiqau@gmail.com covering U-01, U-02, U-03,
      U-05, U-06, and U-12.
- [ ] Keep the sent message and any reply.
- [ ] If no reply arrives, follow the conservative default in section 7 and say
      so in the internal submission note.

### 8.4 Verify the artifacts themselves

- [ ] Video is landscape, ≤120.0 s, plays end-to-end, audio intelligible.
- [ ] Video container is MP4 or MOV and is not audio-only.
- [ ] PDF opens on a clean machine, contains all nine mandatory sections, and
      states the project stage honestly.
- [ ] Budget, if present, has item, quantity, unit cost, total, currency.
- [ ] PDF, video, and app agree on scope, status, timeline, and impact.
- [ ] No fabricated traction, partnerships, user numbers, or official
      integrations.
- [ ] Personal data is limited to the four required fields plus contact email.

### 8.5 Send and archive

- [ ] Send from a monitored address; request confirmation.
- [ ] Confirm the attachment names and sizes in the sent message.
- [ ] Archive the exact sent files, the email, and the dated rules screenshots.
- [ ] Record the send time in Asia/Almaty and the delivery evidence.
- [ ] Do not delete the archive until results are announced.
